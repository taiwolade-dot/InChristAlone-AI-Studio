About InChristAlone AI Studio

InChristAlone AI Studio is a Christian AI platform built to equip pastors, churches, and theological institutions with practical, AI-powered tools for ministry, research, and content creation.

Our vision is to become a one-stop AI Prompt Engine platform for the global Christian community - helping ministers prepare sermons, build church websites, manage congregations, track academic work, and create media content, all in one place.

What We Offer

  • AI Prompt Engine for sermons, Bible studies, and ministry content
  • Real AI image, video, music, and voice generation
  • Website generation for churches and ministries
  • Church membership management
  • Seminary and thesis tracking tools
  • A marketplace for premium ministry prompt packs

Founder

InChristAlone AI Studio was founded by Rev. Taiwo Jacob Lade, Senior Pastor and founder of InChristAlone Digital & AI Solutions, built entirely to serve the Nigerian Baptist Convention community and beyond.
